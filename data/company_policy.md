# Sample Company Policy (demo data)

This file is made-up sample content so you can try the chatbot right away.

## Leave policy
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days
can be carried forward to the next year. Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year,
and a medical certificate is needed for absences longer than 3 days.

## Remote work
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone.

## Expense claims
Submit travel and meal expenses within 30 days with receipts. Meals while travelling
are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day. Claims above 10,000 rupees need a second approval.
